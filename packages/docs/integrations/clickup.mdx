---
title: ClickUp Triggers
description: Configure ClickUp webhook inputs that publish Event occurrences.
---

Connect ClickUp and attach a ClickUp source Trigger to an Event. Select the
workspace resources and match rules in the browser editor. Matching signed
webhook deliveries produce validated durable occurrences; duplicate delivery
IDs are ignored.

Inspect safe delivery outcomes with `list_trigger_webhook_activity` and
`list_webhook_deliveries`. See [Event payloads and conditions](/integrations/events)
and [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/events).
