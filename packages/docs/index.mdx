title: Factorize
description: A development environment for your agent.
---

Factorize is a development environment for your agent. Connect the services it
needs, approve just the access it should have, and see and revoke that access
in [Factorize](https://app.factorize.sh).

- **Use an API.** Your agent calls the APIs of connected services through MCP.
  Factorize uses the credentials you connected without handing them to the agent.
- **Give a VM access.** Give a supported workload its own scoped Factorize
  access, separate from the agent connection.
- **React to an event.** Use webhook or schedule Triggers, optionally combining
  inputs and authorized lookups before publishing an Event occurrence.
  Factorize publishes; the subscriber decides what to do with the occurrence.

[Try one of the three pathways](/quickstart) or [look up MCP and REST](/mcp/tools).
